description: Evaluate and import rights-eligible source assets
---

Load the `pinaka-project` skill. Treat `$1` as the project ID and the remaining arguments as the sourcing
request. Read project status and grounding first. Prefer authorized client assets, then approved sources.
Do not download or import an item until its origin-page licence evidence and intended-use eligibility pass.
No paid or bulk operation is permitted by this command.
